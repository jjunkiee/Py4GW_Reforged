# Component Register

Status: current — fan-in, fan-out and line counts generated by
[tools/build_graph.py](tools/build_graph.py) into [graph.json](graph.json) on 2026-09-05
against clean `main` at `8dfdcaf2`. Verdicts written against current source.

One row per component. A **component** is a namespace (area plus its first subdirectory,
where ownership actually sits here), plus an individual row for any module large or
distinctive enough to be a component in its own right.

Read [README.md](README.md) first — in particular the reachability rule. **Zero fan-in is
not evidence of dead code** for anything under `Widgets/`, `Bots/` or most of `Sources/`.

- **fan-in / fan-out** on a namespace row are *cross-namespace* edges. On a module row they
  are that module's own totals.
- **Verdicts** are exactly one of `Keep` · `Improve in place` · `Fold into <owner>` ·
  `Split` · `Remove`.
- **Confidence** is `verified` (read at source), `inferred` (reasoned from the graph), or
  `unresolved`.
- **Flags** T1–T5 are defined in [README.md](README.md#how-to-read-the-register) and
  collected per component in [Flags](#flags).

not re-checked retain the evidence citation they were given and are marked accordingly.

## Known Unknowns

Register-specific. The structural unknowns are in
[dependency-map.md](dependency-map.md#known-unknowns).

1. **Runtime enablement.** A `.widget` marker makes a module discoverable, not enabled.
   Enabled state is per-account `Settings` data written at runtime. Every `Keep` on a
   widget is a statement about ownership, not usage.
2. **`Sources/frenkeyLib/LootEx` disposition.** Reachability is verified (zero external
   importers, 18,447 loc). Whether it is retired, migrated or revived is a project decision
   about a contributor's code, and the autopsy exists because that exact call was made
   wrongly once. No verdict recorded.
3. **Which of the four mod models is canonical.** Requires live item-mod evidence from the
   client, not gathered here.
4. **`Shards Of Orr BT test.py` intent.** 3,386 identical lines is a fact; intent is not.
   If the author is bisecting a live bug, the correct action is a `_disabled` rename, not
   removal.
5. **`Legacy/System Monitor.py`.** The legacy copy is *larger* than its named successor.
   The 2,160-line delta was not reviewed; do not delete on supersession alone.
6. **`Legacy/NativeContext.py` salvage value.** Determining what it has that
   `Py4GWCoreLib/Context.py` lacks requires native-layout verification.
7. **`Sources/modular_data/prebuilt` and `Sources/Nikon_Scripts` farm scripts.** No
   importer, no path-loader, no string reference. Unreferenced, not provably dead.
8. **In-client impact of the `UIManagerExtensions.py:153` `NameError`.** Verified in source
   and by execution; how often the fallback branch is reached on a live client is unknown.
9. **The `Bot_Factory.py` tkinter-in-render-thread hazard** is a source-level inference; no
   hang or crash was reproduced.
10. **No live verification was performed for this baseline.** Every claim above is
    source-level or offline-execution evidence. The offline test suite ran (11 pass, 7 fail; see
    `../records/refactor-phase-0-waypoint.md`);
    nothing was validated against an injected client. The autopsy's distinction between
    static conformance and product proof applies to this entire register.
